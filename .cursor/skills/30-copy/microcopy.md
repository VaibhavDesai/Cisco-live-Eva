# Microcopy workflow

## Use for
- short UI strings
- section subtitles
- empty-state titles and one-line descriptions
- status and confirmation text

## Rules
- Keep it concise and direct.
- Prefer action-oriented phrasing.
- Use plain language and avoid jargon.
- Keep one idea per line.
- Use sentence case.

## Avoid
- Generic filler language
- Vague wording with no next step
- Overly clever phrasing that hides meaning

## Rewrite pattern

1. Identify user goal in context.
2. Reduce to essential words.
3. Keep tone confident and helpful.
4. Add a next step when useful.

## Examples

- current: "You have successfully completed the setup process."
  proposed: "Setup is complete"
  why: "Shorter and clearer"

- current: "There are currently no items available to display."
  proposed: "No items yet"
  why: "Concise and scannable"

- net-new:
  proposed: "Saved. Your changes are live"
  why: "Confirms outcome and value"
